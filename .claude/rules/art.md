# Art

- All art is SVG generated by `tools/gen_art.py` (python3, no dependencies), cutscene portraits included. `gen_art.py` is the entry point (`main()`, whose call order is the manifest's order); the art is in `tools/art/` by kind, with the shared toolkit (palette, `P`, the `Art` canvas, `save()`, `manifest`, helpers used by several modules) in `tools/art/core.py`. Content modules import from core, and from each other one way only. **Never hand-edit `art/*.svg` or `scripts/core/art_manifest.gd`**: edit the generator and rerun it.
- The generator also writes the manifest, so a new asset needs no manual registration.
- Buildings, walls, gates and wall towers have art per material and level (`Config.piece_art` names them). Huts' materials only change their look.
- A tap picks by the art's pixels (`Config.PICK`), so changing a building's art changes what is clickable.
- Store-listing images under `fastlane/` come from the same icon; `.gdignore` keeps Godot out of that folder.
- **Item and relic icons** (`item_<id>`, `relic_<id>`; `ITEM_ICONS`, `RELIC_ICONS` in `tools/art/icons.py`): a new item or relic needs its icon there. The relic icons stop the generator if a `Config.RELICS` key has none. Item icons always get the item badge, relic icons the relic badge: an item is never a relic.
- Village-coloured art (the co-op heroes' `unit_hero_c<i>`) reads `Config.VILLAGE_COLORS` straight from `config.gd`, so after changing those colours rerun the generator.
- **Banner cloth** (docs/village-design.md §12): every banner, pennant or flag on a village piece, the town hall or the barracks is drawn only through `Art.cloth` in `tools/art/core.py` (`hanging_banner` uses it), always crimson, never in a material's colour. It makes `save()` write the sprite's grey `<name>_flag` companion, which the game tints with the village colour in co-op. Cloth drawn by hand gets no companion and stays crimson for every village. Ruins' rags and shields are not cloth in this sense.
